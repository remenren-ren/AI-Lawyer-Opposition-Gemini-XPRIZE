AI Lawyer Opposition
Advanced 18-Dimension Review - User Guide

PURPOSE

Advanced 18-Dimension Review is a deeper optional weakness-review mode. It is
designed for matters where preserving each legal perspective and receiving a
complete written report are more important than minimizing API use.

HOW TO OPEN IT

1. Open either the Online or Offline application.
2. Import or enter the complete matter.
3. Add and verify at least one permitted model provider.
4. Click AI Weakness Scan or Scan Weaknesses.
5. Choose Advanced 18-Dimension Review for independent whole-matter reports.
   In the Online application, choose the purple Contextual 18-Dimension
   Challenge when every dimension should also test both sides' current
   arguments and evidence against the complete matter.

Choosing Standard Scan returns to the existing weakness-card workflow without
changing its established logic.

WHAT THE ADVANCED MODE DOES

- The complete prepared matter is read independently through each of 18 legal
  review dimensions.
- Each dimension uses a separate model call and creates a separate full report.
- A later dimension does not receive or copy the previous dimension's report.
- The model is instructed not to use internal P1/P2/D1/D2 labels or a
  sentence-by-sentence software decomposition.
- Each report includes whole-matter understanding, full dimension reasoning,
  positive-side analysis, negative-side analysis, important weaknesses,
  questions, and possible response or cure paths.

OUTPUT

Every run creates a new folder under:

runs\advanced_18_dimension_review_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS

The folder contains:

- 00_prepared_case.txt: the exact prepared text used for the review;
- 00_INDEX.md: index of all dimension reports;
- 00_manifest.json: structured combined result record;
- 00_COMPLETE_18_REPORTS.html: complete highlighted delivery report;
- one JSON file and one Markdown report for every dimension.

Use Open Combined Report after completion to inspect the highlighted findings
and all 18 full reports in one document.

PRIVACY

Advanced Review sends the complete prepared matter once per dimension. It must
not be started unless that transmission is authorized.

- Online with Data Redaction: the prepared redacted matter is used.
- Online without Data Redaction: the original matter is used after explicit
  confirmation.
- Offline External aid after redaction: the prepared redacted matter is used.
- Offline Authorized cloud expert: the authorized original matter is used.
- Offline Local-only confidentiality: only a verified local or private model
  route is accepted.

MODEL AND TOKEN LIMITS

The 18 calls are independent. This prevents one long dimension report from
using the output allowance of the other dimensions and avoids one malformed
large JSON response destroying the entire run.

Independent calls do not remove provider account limits. HTTP 429 means that
the provider's request-rate or account quota has been reached. The software
tries the next verified provider for the affected dimension. Reports already
completed remain saved. Only the failed or unfinished dimension needs later
review.

TWO COMPLEMENTARY WEAKNESS-SCAN MODES

AI Lawyer Opposition provides two complementary weakness-scan perspectives.
They are deliberately different rather than duplicate functions. A law firm
can use both, or select the perspective that best matches its preferred review
method. A deployed service can therefore be configured around the firm's
intake process, litigation workflow, confidentiality requirements, and model
environment.

COMPARISON TABLE

| Comparison point | Online Standard Scan | Offline Standard Scan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material reviewed | Complete matter plus the positive-side arguments and evidence and the negative-side arguments and evidence | Primarily reads the complete original or prepared matter directly |
| Analysis orientation | Tests weaknesses in positions that the parties have already formed; operates like a focused challenge within an active dispute | Reconstructs and understands the matter as a whole before each selected dimension independently searches for weaknesses |
| Dimension mechanism | Sends the selected dimensions as structured review tasks and automatically divides them into smaller calls if output capacity is reached | Also performs a structured whole-matter review across the selected dimensions and divides the work into smaller calls when necessary |
| Multiple-model handling | Combines findings returned by the verified model providers | Also combines successful model findings, with additional routing controls for local models, private models, and confidentiality modes |
| Result presentation | Produces concise positive-side and negative-side weakness cards directly and removes explicit no-material-weakness records from the final visible cards | Converts the model findings into complete internal candidate records before presenting them as reusable weakness cards |
| Practical review experience | More direct and targeted when both sides' arguments, evidence, exceptions, or conditions are already visible | More open-ended and better suited to discovering weaknesses before the matter has been organised into fixed opposing positions |

Mode 1 - Whole-Matter Weakness Discovery

- This is the principal perspective of the Offline Standard Scan.
- The model reads the complete prepared matter directly and searches for
  weaknesses across the overall facts, relationships, proof gaps, legal
  elements, and competing possible interpretations.
- It does not depend on the software's previously extracted positive and
  negative argument or evidence frames.
- It is especially useful at first intake, when the lawyer wants an open-ended
  review of the client's entire story before fixing a litigation position.

Mode 2 - Opponent-Position Contextual Challenge

- This is the principal perspective of the Online Standard Scan.
- The model reads the complete matter together with the positive-side and
  negative-side arguments and evidence already presented in the workspace.
- It tests each side's introduced conditions, assumptions, exceptions, legal
  propositions, and factual inferences against the complete case rather than
  reviewing an argument in isolation.
- It is especially useful after the parties' positions are visible, because it
  can identify the exact premise on which an opponent's argument depends and
  explain why the wider record may not satisfy that premise.

For example, a party may rely on privity of contract. Contextual Challenge can
test that position against the other side's alleged variation, novation,
waiver, intervention, instructions, acceptance of benefits, and conduct. The
software does not assume that any such doctrine succeeds. It identifies the
condition being introduced, checks it against the supplied facts, and explains
where the argument remains exposed or requires professional verification.

The two modes answer different professional questions:

- Whole-Matter Weakness Discovery asks: "What weaknesses exist anywhere in
  this case when the complete matter is read afresh?"
- Opponent-Position Contextual Challenge asks: "Given the arguments and
  conditions already raised by both sides, which of those positions becomes
  vulnerable when tested against the complete matter?"

WHY ONLINE STANDARD SCAN OFTEN FEELS MORE PRECISE

The difference is not merely model intelligence. The Online Standard Scan is
asked a materially different question because its input contains the complete
matter and both sides' current arguments and evidence. The model can therefore
identify the premise on which a particular position depends, compare that
premise with the complete record, and state which missing fact, evidentiary
bridge, legal condition, exception, or causal link makes the position
vulnerable.

The standard online workflow normally places all selected dimensions into one
shared structured whole-case call for each verified provider. It divides the
dimensions into smaller calls only when output capacity requires it. Every
finding must identify the affected side and explain the weakness by reference
to supplied facts. A dimension may return no finding, and explicit
no-material-weakness records are removed from the final visible cards.

By contrast, the Offline whole-matter model prompt primarily receives the
original complete matter without depending on the prepared positive and
negative side frames. It is therefore more exploratory and tends to expose
case-wide factual, evidentiary, element, causation, and narrative gaps. In
local-only mode, the secure local engine additionally reviews the positive and
negative perspectives in separate passes.

The practical distinction is:

- Online contextual review asks which existing party position fails when it
  is tested against the complete matter.
- Offline whole-matter discovery asks what vulnerabilities exist anywhere in
  the case when the matter is read afresh.

The side frames remain allegations and working positions, not established
facts. Poorly prepared or inaccurate side frames can cause a contextual scan
to focus on the wrong premise, so professional review of the input frames
remains necessary.

This two-perspective design gives a law firm a practical deployment choice.
Some firms may prefer open whole-matter discovery during client intake. Others
may prefer targeted contextual challenge once pleadings, submissions, or an
opponent's theory are available. Firms can retain both and apply them at
different stages of the same matter.

These two perspectives are separate from the Standard-versus-Advanced depth
choice below. Standard Scan produces interactive weakness cards. Advanced
18-Dimension Review makes every dimension reread the complete prepared matter
independently and preserves a full report for each dimension.

PURPLE ONLINE 18-DIMENSION BUTTON

The Online application retains the existing Standard Scan and the existing
Advanced 18-Dimension Review. It also provides a separate purple Contextual
18-Dimension Challenge button. Nothing in the original two workflows is
replaced.

The purple workflow applies the Online Standard Scan perspective at advanced
depth. Each of the 18 dimensions independently receives:

- the original complete case;
- the current positive-side arguments and evidence; and
- the current negative-side arguments and evidence.

Each dimension writes its own full free-form report and then extracts only the
supported surface-card titles and previews. The side frames are treated as
positions to test, not as established facts. The reviewer checks introduced
conditions, assumptions, exceptions, factual bridges, and legal propositions
against the complete matter. It does not replace the gold whole-matter review,
which remains available when a firm wants each dimension to approach the case
without relying on the prepared side frames.

The Offline application continues to show the original Standard Scan and gold
Advanced 18-Dimension Review only.

DRAGGING A WEAKNESS INTO SINGLE-POINT REVIEW

An Advanced 18-Dimension weakness card can be dragged into either the Standard
Single-Point Review window or the Advanced Single-Point 2R window. Drop the
card anywhere inside the relevant Single-Point Review window.

The selected-point field receives the card conclusion, its supported preview,
the review dimension, and the source model. The Point belongs to selector is
set automatically to the side weakened by the card. The lawyer can edit or
narrow the inserted text before starting the single-point workflow.

Dropping a card into Single-Point Review does not remove it from the weakness
card list and does not change the original full 18-dimension report.

STANDARD OR ADVANCED

Choose Standard Scan when you need:

- faster interactive weakness cards;
- coordinated results in the existing case workflow;
- lower model use;
- direct card inspection and reuse.

Choose Advanced 18-Dimension Review when you need:

- every dimension to reread the full matter independently;
- 18 preserved professional reports;
- deeper coverage with less risk of dimensions suppressing one another;
- isolated failures and auditable source reports;
- a combined highlighted report for lawyer review.

SYNTHETIC ANALOGUE CASE DIRECTORY BUTTONS

The Synthetic Analogue Case Directory has two separate model controls.

Scan Selected Cases (Max 5)

- This is the broader fictional-case weakness-card scan.
- Select between one and five fictional matters. On Windows, hold Ctrl while
  clicking separate matters, or hold Shift to select a continuous range. A
  normal click selects one matter and clears the previous selection.
- The selected matters form one user batch, but they are reviewed sequentially
  by matter and dimension.
- Every enabled dimension independently rereads the complete fictional matter
  and writes a natural free-form internal analysis without a card template.
- A short second pass reads that completed analysis and extracts only the
  affected side, outside-card title, and one-sentence preview. Opening the card
  displays the original free-form report, not a reformatted explanation.
- Each enabled dimension therefore uses approximately two provider calls. With
  18 dimensions, one fictional matter uses approximately 36 calls; five matters
  may use approximately 180 calls. The confirmation dialog shows the estimate.
- The five-matter ceiling controls run time, provider cost, rate-limit exposure,
  and output reliability. It prevents several detailed matters from competing
  for one response limit and isolates a failed or malformed case response.
- Results from completed matters are combined and locally deduplicated at the
  end of the selected batch.

18D Opposition for Selected Case

- This is a deep courtroom-style attack-and-defence workflow, not the multi-case
  weakness-card scan.
- Select exactly one fictional matter before using this button.
- The selected matter enters the complete 18-dimension, two-round opposition
  workflow so its facts, two sides, responses, and follow-up attacks remain one
  coherent case narrative.
- Other fictional matters must be opened separately. Combining several matters
  would mix independent narratives and multiply the calls and output volume.

For both controls, only the selected fictional material is transmitted to the
verified provider. The original client matter and local skeleton are not sent.

DEMO NARRATION

"The blue button lets us select up to five fictional analogues and queue them
for weakness discovery. For each analogue, every enabled dimension independently
rereads the complete fictional matter and writes its analysis freely. A short
second pass extracts only the title shown on the outside card; opening the card
shows the original free-form report instead of a fixed explanation template.
The gold button is different: it takes one selected fictional matter into the
complete 18-dimension, two-round attack-and-defence workflow. In either case,
the original client matter remains local."

PROFESSIONAL REVIEW

All outputs are legal-preparation and drafting references. They may contain
incorrect factual inferences, legal propositions, citations, or jurisdictional
assumptions. A qualified person must check the original record, applicable law,
authorities, and final wording before use.
