## Nido Tactic Package

Auto assignment: positive-side weaknesses are assigned to the opposing side; negative-side weaknesses are assigned to the positive side.

## Tactics For Positive Side
1. [Cross-Domain Weapon] Target: the opposing side's customer service chat record, used to prove notice timing.
   - Check source, screenshot completeness, platform backend consistency, and privacy or unauthorized evidence-collection risk.

## Tactics For Negative Side
1. [Procedural Defect] Target: the positive side's unboxing video, used to prove when the defect was discovered.
   - Check whether the video is continuous, whether timestamps match the transaction timeline, and whether packaging and seals are visible.

## Usage
Import this package back into the main battlefield. The software should append it to the argument boxes and must not overwrite the original case.
